# Success Criterion 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)

> Verbatim copy of the normative text. Do not edit; replace the whole file
> when W3C publishes a new edition and bump `edition` / `captured`.

The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of
at least 4.5:1, except for the following:

**Large Text**
: Large-scale text and images of large-scale text have a contrast ratio of
  at least 3:1;

**Incidental**
: Text or images of text that are part of an inactive user interface
  component, that are pure decoration, that are not visible to anyone, or
  that are part of a picture that contains significant other visual
  content, have no contrast requirement.

**Logotypes**
: Text that is part of a logo or brand name has no contrast requirement.

## Definitions used (normative)

**large scale (text)**
: with at least 18 point or 14 point bold or font size that would yield
  equivalent size for Chinese, Japanese and Korean (CJK) fonts

**contrast ratio**
: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 is the relative luminance of the
  lighter of the colors, and L2 is the relative luminance of the darker of
  the colors.

The notes under both definitions are informative and are not copied; read
them at the links above.
