## Applicability

As axe-core 4.13.0 `frame-title-unique`: elements matching `frame[title], iframe[title]`, filtered by axe's `frame-title-has-text-matches`.

## Expectation

As axe-core 4.13.0 `frame-title-unique`: Frames must have a unique title attribute.
