## Applicability

As axe-core 4.13.0 `scrollable-region-focusable`: elements matching `*:not(select,textarea)`, filtered by axe's `scrollable-region-focusable-matches`.

## Expectation

As axe-core 4.13.0 `scrollable-region-focusable`: Scrollable region must have keyboard access.
