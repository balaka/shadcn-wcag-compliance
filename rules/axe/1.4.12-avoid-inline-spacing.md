## Applicability

As axe-core 4.13.0 `avoid-inline-spacing`: elements matching `[style]`, filtered by axe's `is-visible-on-screen-matches`.

## Expectation

As axe-core 4.13.0 `avoid-inline-spacing`: Inline text spacing must be adjustable with custom stylesheets.
