## Applicability

As axe-core 4.13.0 `aria-tooltip-name`: elements matching `[role="tooltip"]`, filtered by axe's `no-naming-method-matches`.

## Expectation

As axe-core 4.13.0 `aria-tooltip-name`: ARIA tooltip nodes must have an accessible name.

Checks (any of): `has-visible-text`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `non-empty-title`.
