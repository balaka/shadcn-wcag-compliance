## Applicability

This rule applies to any element with its own visible text — a text node
child that is not only whitespace, or the value of a `textarea` or of an
`input` that shows its value as text (not a radio's or checkbox's value) —
that is [visible][] (not `display: none`, not `visibility: hidden`, a
non-zero box, no `opacity: 0` on it or an ancestor), is not part of an
inactive user interface component (`disabled`, `aria-disabled="true"`,
`data-disabled` on it or an ancestor), and for which axe's
`color-contrast` cannot settle the contrast:

- the text is one character long, or only punctuation, symbols or
  characters outside the Basic Multilingual Plane;
- on the element or any ancestor, a `::before` and `::after` with a
  background together cover more than a quarter of the text element's
  area (axe's own threshold for giving up);
- axe's `getBackgroundColor` finds no background (overlap, gradient,
  image, SVG) or its foreground equals its background.

Not applicable: text whose characters are drawn elsewhere by design —
input-otp's real input (`input[data-input-otp]`), transparent, with the
digits rendered in the slots next to it (WCAG 1.4.3 Incidental: not
visible to anyone; the slots are checked themselves).

## Expectation

For each test target, the [contrast ratio][] between the text color as
drawn and every backdrop the text may be drawn on is at least 4.5:1, or at
least 3:1 for [large-scale text][] (24px, or 18.66px at weight 700).

- The text color is the computed `color`, the `fill` for SVG text, or every
  stop of a gradient clipped to the text (`background-clip: text`, a
  shimmer). Opacity of the element and its ancestors is applied.
- The backdrops are the layers under the text, from the element up to the
  first opaque background: background colors; every stop of a background
  gradient; the background of a `::before`/`::after` larger than a quarter
  of its element, when its box covers the text's center (when its box
  cannot be read, measured both with and without it; a smaller one, such
  as a 2px underline, is not a backdrop); and any translucent element
  drawn on top of the text at its center. White when nothing below is
  opaque (WCAG's note to "contrast ratio").
- Every text color is measured on every backdrop; the worst pair decides.
  The ratio is compared exactly and shown rounded down (4.499 shows 4.49).

Cannot tell, for a person: the text is covered at its center by an opaque
element or an image; the text is drawn in exactly the color of its
backdrop (hidden on purpose, or invisible).

## Background

axe's `color-contrast` is the right executor wherever it can see the
colors; its own documentation lists the cases it hands to a person. In a
design system those cases are not rare: the 445 stories of the shadcn
examples gave 155 of them on 2026-10-06, and most are measurable.

### Assumptions

- A loading shimmer is text: its faintest moment is measured. Whether a
  person accepts a moment of low contrast during loading is a decision, not
  a measurement.
- Text under a pseudo element may or may not be covered by it; both are
  measured and the worse counts.

## Test Cases

Numbers computed by `fromColors` in src/rules/1.4.3-text-contrast.ts.

### Passed

#### Passed Example 1

Near-black on white: #0a0a0a on #ffffff = 19.79:1.

```html
<kbd style="color: #0a0a0a; background: #fff">K</kbd>
```

#### Passed Example 2

Large bold text needs 3:1: #949494 on #ffffff = 3.03:1, 24px bold.

```html
<p style="color: #949494; background: #fff; font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700">↑</p>
```

### Failed

#### Failed Example 1

shadcn's Kbd (muted on muted), one character: #737373 on #f5f5f5 = 4.34:1,
needs 4.5:1.

```html
<kbd style="color: #737373; background: #f5f5f5; font-size: 12px">⌘</kbd>
```

#### Failed Example 2

A loading shimmer in shadcn's Attachment: the title is painted by a
gradient from oklch(0.145 0 0) to oklch(0.145 0 0 / 0.2); at the faintest
stop #cecece on #ffffff = 1.57:1.

```html
<span style="background: linear-gradient(110deg, oklch(0.145 0 0), oklch(0.145 0 0 / 0.6), oklch(0.145 0 0 / 0.2)); background-clip: text; -webkit-text-fill-color: transparent">design-system.zip</span>
```

#### Failed Example 3

The same #949494 on #ffffff = 3.03:1 at normal size, needs 4.5:1.

```html
<p style="color: #949494; background: #fff; font-size: 14px">→</p>
```

### Inapplicable

#### Inapplicable Example 1

The text belongs to a disabled control.

```html
<button disabled style="color: #ccc; background: #fff">K</button>
```

#### Inapplicable Example 2

input-otp's real input: white on white by design, the digits are drawn in
the slots.

```html
<input data-input-otp value="123456" style="color: #fff; background: #fff">
```

#### Inapplicable Example 3

axe settles it: two words of dark text on white — axe's `color-contrast`
measures this, not this rule.

```html
<p style="color: #0a0a0a; background: #fff">Two words</p>
```
